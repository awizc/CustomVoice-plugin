CustomVoice does not install or launch the OBS application. These components
are used only during a user-selected application broadcast, once that window
is detected in the foreground covering its monitor. The capture session then
stays attached across Alt-Tab and reads only that game's rendering surface.
Existing capture ownership is respected. Stopping the broadcast signals the
hook to stop capturing; the OBS hook library may remain loaded until the game
exits, as designed upstream. No system-wide Vulkan layer is installed.
